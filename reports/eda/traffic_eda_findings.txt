TRAFFIC DATA EDA - FINDINGS
===========================

Dataset
-------
Records: 42,756
Columns: 31
Date range: 2012-01-08 to 2021-05-09
Distinct dates: 608
Exact duplicate rows: 0

Data quality
------------
No negative values were found in the numeric hourly readings.
Missing values are concentrated in afternoon and evening columns.
The 12 afternoon/evening columns have 253 missing values each.
The 4:00-5:00 AM column has the highest IQR outlier count: 4,975.
IQR outliers are candidates for investigation, not automatically errors.

Preliminary traffic observations
--------------------------------
The 3:00-4:00 AM column has a mean of approximately 117.62.
The 4:00-5:00 PM column has a mean of approximately 661.12.
These are source-column comparisons, not a fully validated hourly profile.

Extreme-value investigation
---------------------------
The highest recorded hourly-column value is 10,532.
It occurs on the Bronx River Parkway, southbound, on 2015-09-13.
The same segment has 9 matching records across 9 distinct dates,
from 2015-09-12 through 2015-09-20.
The cause and measurement units of the high readings have not been verified.

Limitations
-----------
Hourly source labels have inconsistent formatting and require validation.
Zero readings may be legitimate and have not been replaced.
No outliers or missing values have been modified.
Traffic trends and causes should not be inferred until validation is complete.
